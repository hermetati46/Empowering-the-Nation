Course Overview

Our comprehensive Sewing course is designed to equip students with essential skills and knowledge to create and alter garments effectively. You'll gain the confidence and competence to bring your creative visions to life through sewing.

Course Content

Master essential sewing skills through our structured curriculum designed for progressive learning.

Pattern Reading

Master the art of reading and interpreting sewing patterns, understanding symbols, and making adjustments for a perfect fit.

Fabric Selection

Learn to choose the right fabrics for different projects, understanding texture, weight, and drape characteristics.

Machine Operation

Master various sewing machines, from basic to advanced models, including maintenance and troubleshooting.

Garment Construction

Create professional-quality garments with proper fitting, finishing techniques, and attention to detail.

6 Months
