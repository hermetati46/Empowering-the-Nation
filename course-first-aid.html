Course Overview

This comprehensive First Aid course is designed to equip students with essential skills and knowledge to respond effectively to medical emergencies. You'll gain the confidence and competence to provide immediate care that can save lives and prevent further injury.

Course Content

Comprehensive training covering all essential first aid skills and emergency response techniques.

Scene Assessment

Learn to quickly evaluate emergency situations, identify hazards, and ensure scene safety before providing care.

CPR & AED

Master cardiopulmonary resuscitation techniques and automated external defibrillator operation for cardiac emergencies.

Bleeding Control

Techniques for controlling severe bleeding, wound care, and proper bandaging methods to prevent blood loss.

Injury Treatment

Treatment protocols for burns, fractures, sprains, and other common injuries encountered in emergency situations.

Duration

6 Months

Comprehensive training program with hands-on practice sessions.
